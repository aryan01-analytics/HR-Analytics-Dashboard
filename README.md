## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most costly challenges faced by modern organisations. This **HR Analytics Dashboard** was built in **Power BI** to give HR teams and leadership a clear, interactive view of workforce data — enabling them to:

- 🔍 Identify **why** employees are leaving
- 🎯 Pinpoint **which roles, departments, and salary bands** are most at risk
- 📈 Take **data-backed decisions** to improve retention and performance

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Install Power BI Desktop from https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop
2. Clone this repository
3. Open Hr_Analytics_Dashboard.pbix in Power BI Desktop
4. Use the department tabs (Human Resources / R&D / Sales) to filter data
5. Click any chart to cross-filter other visuals
6. Hover over data points for detailed tooltips

## 💡 Key Business Insights and Recommendations

| Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 69% attrition in lowest salary bracket | Review and revise compensation for entry-level roles |
| 26–35 age group leaves the most | Introduce clear career growth paths and promotion policies |
| Lab Technicians have highest attrition | Conduct stay interviews; review workload and recognition |
| Attrition peaks at Year 1 and Year 5 | Strengthen onboarding and mid-career development plans |
| Life Sciences graduates exit the most | Offer skill development, research opportunities, and mentoring |
